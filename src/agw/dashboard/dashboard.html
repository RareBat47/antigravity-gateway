📖 Method B: How to Extract Gemini Web Session Cookies (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox)
  1. Open your browser with the Google account that has Google AI Pro / Gemini Advanced.
  2. Go to gemini.google.com and confirm you see Gemini Advanced.
  3. Press F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I (Right-click → Inspect) to open Developer Tools.
  4. Click the Application tab (in Firefox, click Storage).
  5. In the left tree, expand Storage → Cookies → click https://gemini.google.com.
  6. Find the cookie named __Secure-1PSID. Double-click its Value column and copy the string.
  7. (Recommended) Also find __Secure-1PSIDTS and copy its value.
  8. Paste either format in the box below:
    • Full: __Secure-1PSID=YOUR_VALUE; __Secure-1PSIDTS=YOUR_TS_VALUE
    • Or simply paste the raw __Secure-1PSID string alone!
